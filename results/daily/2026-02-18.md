#### Theoretical Insight: LORO ≥ HC + LOCO < HC Dissociation

- **LOCO (cross-subject, leave-one-color-out)**: HC > CVD interpolation — CVD geometry is distorted
- Dissociation = CVD has color representations but distorted color space geometry → Phase 3 neural justification
