Date: 2026-02-09
Status: ✅ COMPLETE

Motivation:
-----------
User observed MDS analysis results and hypothesized that PCA on raw data
might improve performance by filtering low-variance noise components.

Analysis:
---------
Tested multiple PCA configurations on raw amplitudes:
• Variance-based: 95%, 90%, 85%, 80% (17-31 components)
• Fixed: 50, 100, 150 components
• Compared with Raw and Procrustes baselines

Key Results:
------------
❌ PCA Shows LIMITED Benefit:
   • RDM: 0.004 (raw) → 0.043 (best PCA) → 0.381 (Procrustes)
   • Decoding: 0.131 (raw) → 0.172 (best PCA) → 0.592 (Procrustes)
   • PCA vs Raw: +0.041 improvement (70% positive)
   • Procrustes vs PCA: +0.420 advantage (100% positive)

✅ Procrustes Remains Superior:
   • 11× more effective than best PCA
   • 100% cases show Procrustes > PCA for decoding
   • Addresses geometric misalignment (PCA cannot)

Performance Comparison:
-----------------------
| Method      | RDM    | Decoding | vs Raw  | vs Procrustes |
|-------------|--------|----------|---------|---------------|
| Raw         | 0.004  | 0.131    | -       | -88%          |
| Best PCA    | 0.043  | 0.172    | +0.041  | -71%          |
| Procrustes  | 0.381  | 0.592    | +0.461  | -             |

Why PCA Fails:
--------------
1. Geometric Misalignment Dominates
   → Between-run variance 16× larger than signal
   → PCA doesn't correct rotation/reflection

2. Signal-Noise Mixing in Principal Components
   → Color signal not concentrated in top PCs
   → Can't separate signal from noise effectively

3. Wrong Problem Addressed
   → PCA solves: "Too many noisy dimensions"
   → Actual problem: "Runs in different coordinate systems"
   → Need ALIGNMENT, not dimensionality reduction

Best PCA Config:
----------------
• Variance 85% (~20 components)
• Decoding: 0.142 (still poor)
• But optimal config varies by subject

Comparison with Procrustes:
----------------------------
Win Rate (Procrustes vs Best PCA):
• RDM reliability: 82.5% (33/40 pairs)
• Decoding accuracy: 100% (40/40 pairs)

No single case where PCA outperforms Procrustes!
