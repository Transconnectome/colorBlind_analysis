# Behavioral summary — exp1 (baseline) + exp2 (filters)

**Scope**: JND (8 hue-pair discrimination thresholds) and RSVP-8AFC (color identification).
**Subjects**: HC = sub-01–07 (JND n=7; RSVP n=4: sub-01,03,06,07). CVD = sub-08 (deutan), sub-09 (protan).
**Conditions**: exp1 = no-filter baseline (ses1); exp2 (ses2, sub-08 only) = **window** (deployed macOS Color Filter, subject-tuned intensity, build 26.5.1) and **optimal** (personalized 2-comp pre-image filter).

> Lower JND = better discrimination. z = (value − HC_mean)/HC_sd per pair. exp2 data exist for **sub-08 only**; sub-09 has baseline only.
> Generated by `analyze_exp2_behavior.py` / `consolidate.py`. Single CVD subject, single run per condition — descriptive.

## (1) exp1: CVD − HC difference (the deficit)

**sub-08 (deutan).** The deficit is **localized to 3 pairs**, not global:
- **orange-yellow** (z = +4.2), **yellow-green** (z = +4.3), **yellow-purple** (z = +6.7) — all exceed the HC empirical max.
- The remaining 5 pairs sit within ±1.2 z of HC.
- Mean |z| = 2.24, mean signed z = +1.55 (uniformly elevated, never below HC — at baseline the deficit is one-directional: worse discrimination).
- Same locus in 8AFC: accuracy 0.812 vs HC 0.973 and RT 3.72 s vs HC 2.44 s, with errors concentrated in the yellow–green and purple–magenta regions.

**sub-09 (protan).** Essentially HC-equivalent on this paradigm: JND mean 0.097 (HC 0.104), 8AFC 1.000, RT 1.52 s. Only **green-blue** is mildly elevated (z = +2.4). → sub-09 has little behavioral headroom; its CVD signature is neural/subtle rather than behavioral here.

> Implication for Phase 3: a *behavioral* filter benefit can only be demonstrated in **sub-08**.

## (2) exp2: how each filter changes the deficit

Both filters move sub-08 from a strongly elevated profile (mean |z| 2.24) toward HC (window 0.85, optimal 0.78) and collapse the 8AFC deficit (0.812 → 0.969 for both; RT 3.72 → 2.44 window / 2.08 optimal).

**The 3 baseline-deficit pairs are normalized by both filters:**

| Pair | baseline (z) | window (z) | optimal (z) |
|---|---|---|---|
| orange-yellow | +4.2 | −1.4 | −1.7 |
| yellow-green | +4.3 | −0.2 | +1.5 |
| yellow-purple | +6.7 | −0.7 | +1.4 |

**But neither filter is uniformly HC-faithful** (deviation in *either* direction matters, since the goal is HC-*like* perception, not maximal discriminability):

- Out of HC empirical range (per pair):
  - **baseline**: 3 pairs, all ↑ (deficit) — orange-yellow, yellow-green, yellow-purple.
  - **window**: 1 pair ↑ — green-blue newly mildly worsened (z +1.6).
  - **optimal**: 2 pairs — yellow-green ↑ (z +1.5) and **orange-yellow ↓ over-corrected** (0.046 < HC min 0.064, z −1.7).
- Mean signed z: window −0.25 (slight net over-separation), optimal −0.03 (net-centered on HC).
- Mean |z| (overall closeness to HC): window 0.85 ≈ optimal 0.78 — **tied**.

**Window vs optimal cannot be ranked from this data:**
1. Underpowered — single subject, single run, 8 pairs (Wilcoxon optimal vs window p = 0.84).
2. **Pipeline confound** (per Path A decision): window = OS-level post-display transform, optimal = PsychoPy direct render. A JND win or tie is therefore not interpretable as personalization. The personalization-specific test lives in the cross-swap (own vs other filter) and fMRI prongs.
3. The two filters fail on *different* pairs (window: green-blue ↑; optimal: orange-yellow ↓ over-correction) — distinct error modes, not a clean ordering.

**Robust, reportable claim**: both the deployed macOS filter and the personalized filter restore sub-08's discrimination and identification to near-HC levels, eliminating the catastrophic yellow–green / purple–magenta confusions. The optimal-vs-window comparison is reserved for the mechanism-specific (pipeline-robust) analyses.

## Caveats

- **Block order (sub-08 ses2, interleaved)**: ① window JND → ② optimal 8AFC → ③ optimal JND → ④ window 8AFC. For JND, window preceded optimal (practice favors optimal, yet tied). For 8AFC, optimal preceded window (so optimal's faster RT is *against* the practice gradient; window-last may carry fatigue). Order is not fully separable in n=1.
- Baseline = ses1 (separate session) → cross-session practice/learning inflates the baseline→filter gap.
- Window intensity/subtype was subject-tuned → it is a *configured clinical* comparator, not a naive one. "Optimal ≈ window" therefore means "matches a well-configured deployed filter."
- HC normative bands are unstable (JND n=7, right-skewed, bounded at 0); parametric bands flip on high-variance pairs depending on the distributional assumption. The HC empirical range (min–max) + continuous signed z are reported as the assumption-light summary.
