Structured inverse inference pipeline for CVD distortion modeling and correction. Two parallel data streams (Procrustes-aligned LOCO for per-color vulnerability, SRM-aligned Delta-RDM for pairwise structure) merge into a composite loss function that fits three candidate distortion models (Machado cone-shift, R+C retinal-cortical, 2-Component angular dilation). The pipeline proceeds through model fitting, pre-image inversion, and behavioral validation. Bottom tier shows per-subject results: Sub-08 (mild deutan, R+C fit rho=0.857, p=0.005, 8/8 exact pre-image), Sub-09 (moderate protan, Machado fit rho=0.762, p=0.018, 4/8 pre-image via Machado but 8/8 via 2-Component), Sub-10 (control, all non-significant, specificity pass). Detection converges across models but correction directions diverge.
