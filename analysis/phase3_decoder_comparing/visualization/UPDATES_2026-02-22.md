## Scientific Interpretation

### sub-08 (Deutan) - M-cone deficiency
**FDR-significant distortions:**
1. **blue-purple** (z=6.15, p=7.68e-10) - Strongest effect
   - This is **increased dissimilarity** (RED bar)
   - Unexpected for Deutan (typically shows decreased dissimilarity in confusion pairs)
   - May reflect **compensatory processing** in V2

2. **orange-yellow** (z=5.45, p=5.17e-08)
   - Also **increased dissimilarity** (RED bar)
   - Unexpected direction, but statistically robust

**Interpretation:** Sub-08 shows significant V2 reorganization, but in the *opposite* direction than classic cone-confusion (decreased dissimilarity). This suggests **enhanced differentiation** of specific color pairs, possibly compensatory neural coding.

### sub-09 (Protan) - L-cone deficiency
**No FDR-significant pairs**
- Despite being the most severe CVD type (complete L-cone loss)
- V2 may not show strong *pairwise* distortions for this subject
- Global RDM structure may still differ (not captured by pairwise tests)

### sub-10 (Deutan) - M-cone deficiency
**FDR-significant distortions:**
1. **blue-purple** (z=2.86, p=4.24e-03)
   - Same pair as sub-08, but weaker effect
   - Also **increased dissimilarity** (RED bar)
   - Consistent compensatory pattern across Deutans

## Key Insights

### 1. Deutan Consistency
Both Deutans (sub-08, sub-10) show **blue-purple** as a significant distortion:
- This pair involves **S-cone pathway** (preserved in Deutan)
- May reflect **sharpened S-opponent signal** to compensate for lost M-L information
- Effect size: sub-08 (z=6.15) >> sub-10 (z=2.86)

### 2. Unexpected Direction
Classical CVD prediction: **decreased** dissimilarity for confusion pairs (blue bars)
Observed pattern: **increased** dissimilarity for blue-purple (red bars)

**Possible mechanisms:**
1. **V2 categorical reorganization**: Enhanced S-opponent signal to maintain perceptual boundaries
2. **Compensatory plasticity**: Neural tuning adapts to maximize discriminability of remaining dimensions
3. **Individual variation**: sub-08 may have stronger compensatory mechanisms than sub-10

### 3. FDR Correction Impact
**Before FDR correction:** Many nominally significant pairs (p < 0.05 uncorrected)
**After FDR correction (global q < 0.05):**
- sub-08: 2 pairs (out of 28 tested = 7.1%)
- sub-09: 0 pairs (0%)
- sub-10: 1 pair (3.6%)

**Total significant tests:** 3 out of 84 tests (3 subjects × 28 pairs) = **3.6% discovery rate**

This is **conservative but appropriate** for:
- Small sample size (n=3 CVD, n=7 HC)
- Multiple testing burden (28 pairs per ROI)
- Exploratory analysis without strong priors on specific pairs
