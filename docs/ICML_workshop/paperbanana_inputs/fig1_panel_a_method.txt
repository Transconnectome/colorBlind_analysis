## Structured Phenotype Extraction and Inverse Inference

We extract a geometry-sensitive phenotype from structured fMRI neural representations and invert it into parsimonious, interpretable distortion fields.

### Step 1: Structured Neural Representation
fMRI hue responses in area hV4 are modeled with K=3 half-wave rectified squared sinusoidal basis channels. Weight matrix W maps channels to voxel responses via ridge regression with GCV.

### Step 2: Geometry-Sensitive Phenotype (LOCO Vulnerability)
Leave-one-color-out cross-validation: W is trained on 7 of 8 colors, prediction error for the held-out color is recorded. The per-color errors define an 8-element vulnerability vector v in R^8 capturing which hues are poorly interpolated.

### Step 3: Inverse Inference of Distortion Parameters
Given v, we infer low-dimensional parameters (1-2 DOF) via grid search. Two model families: (a) Cone-based retinal models (Machado 1-DOF cone shift, R+C 2-DOF retinal+cortical), (b) Cortical opponent-axis model (2-Component angular dilation). Parameters selected by minimizing composite loss with 8!=40,320 exact permutation test.

### Step 4: Correction Assessment
Pre-image inversion to derive stimulus-space correction filter. Assess bijectivity (can all 8 colors be exactly restored?). Behavioral 2AFC validation as future test.
